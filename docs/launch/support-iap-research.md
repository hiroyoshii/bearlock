# Optional Support IAP Research

2026-08-31 first pass. This is a broad, shallow benchmark of public App Store listings that mention optional tips, tip jars, voluntary support, or related support purchases. It is based on public App Store pages/search snippets, not hands-on in-app screenshots.

## What Was Checked

- App Store public listing copy.
- App Store version notes where support IAP was introduced.
- Whether copy says features remain free / unchanged.
- Where the support entry appears when the listing says so.
- Whether the app uses a plain support framing, playful product names, or a privacy/mission framing.

## Patterns Observed

1. The dominant placement is Settings or a Support/About screen.
2. The strongest copy says the support purchase unlocks nothing and the app works the same either way.
3. Several apps explicitly pair support with no ads, no subscriptions, no accounts, and local/private data.
4. Plain tier names are common: Small/Medium/Large Tip or Coffee/Lunch/Dinner.
5. Playful product names appear, but the safest examples still keep the explanatory copy direct.
6. Some apps add small thank-you rewards such as badges or alternate icons. This is possible, but it changes the emotional contract.
7. Monthly support subscriptions appear in a few apps, but they add more billing complexity than Bear Lock needs for MVP.
8. Apps in sensitive categories tend to emphasize privacy, no tracking, and no feature differences.

## Future Bear Lock Direction (After Version 1.0)

Version 1.0 ships without In-App Purchases. Keep this research for a future app version after paid-app agreements, tax information, banking information, and StoreKit testing are complete.

Recommended product direction:

- App remains free.
- No ads.
- No account.
- No cloud sync for MVP.
- Optional support is a consumable In-App Purchase.
- Entry point lives low in Settings, not Home.
- Copy clearly says support does not unlock features, change locks, shorten locks, or affect blocking behavior.

Recommended screen copy:

```text
Bear Lockを支える

Bear Lockは無料です。広告、アカウント、サブスクリプションはありません。
気に入ったら、開発を続ける手助けができます。Bear Lockの機能は変わりません。
```

Recommended product-name style:

- Use the observed `Coffee / Lunch / Dinner` pattern for Bear Lock's first support products.
- This avoids making the first tier feel like a "small" or lesser contribution while keeping the purchase meaning easy to understand.
- Product IDs: `com.hiyozoo.bearlock.support.coffee`, `com.hiyozoo.bearlock.support.lunch`, `com.hiyozoo.bearlock.support.dinner`.
- App Store Connect reference names: `Bear Lock Support - Coffee`, `Bear Lock Support - Lunch`, `Bear Lock Support - Dinner`.
- Avoid using `寄付` as the main term in product metadata; use `Support` / `任意サポート` / `開発支援`.
