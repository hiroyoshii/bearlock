# Bear Lock Privacy Policy Draft

Last updated: 2026-08-27

Bear Lock is designed to protect your focus without collecting unnecessary data.

## Data We Do Not Collect

Bear Lock does not collect, sell, or share personal data.

The MVP does not use:

- Accounts.
- Cloud sync.
- Advertising.
- Third-party analytics.
- Tracking.
- Remote crash reporting.
- Server-side storage.

## Screen Time App Selection

Bear Lock uses Apple's Screen Time APIs so you can choose apps or categories to block during scheduled focus windows.

App and category selections are handled through Apple's `FamilyActivityPicker`. Bear Lock stores the resulting selection tokens locally so it can apply your chosen locks. These tokens are stored on your device in the app's App Group container.

## Lock Schedules

Bear Lock stores your lock schedules locally on your device. A schedule may include:

- The lock type.
- Start time.
- End time or duration.
- Recurrence settings.
- A reference to your selected blocked apps or categories.

This information is not uploaded to Bear Lock servers.

## Diagnostics

Bear Lock includes a local Diagnostics screen to help troubleshoot setup and lock behavior. Diagnostics may include:

- Authorization status.
- Number of saved target selections.
- Scheduled rule count.
- Active lock status.
- Safety policy status.
- Recent local app events.
- Local App Group path.

Diagnostics are stored locally. If you contact support, you may choose to share a screenshot of the Diagnostics screen.

## Permissions

Bear Lock requests Screen Time / Family Controls permission to provide app blocking. You can manage or revoke permissions in iOS Settings.
