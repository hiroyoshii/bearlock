# Bear Lock Landing Page Outline

## Goal

Create a simple product page that explains Bear Lock's value clearly enough for early users, reviewers, and entitlement/application context.

## Target Audience

- People who lose time to distracting apps.
- Students, makers, and professionals who want protected focus windows.
- People who want bedtime or wind-down app blocking without an ongoing productivity dashboard.

## First View

Headline:

Bear Lock

Supporting copy:

Choose the apps. Set the wake time. Bear Lock keeps distractions outside until the timer ends.

Primary CTA:

Join the early test list

Secondary CTA:

Read privacy notes

Visual:

Use the app icon or sleeping bear state visual as the first-viewport brand signal. The page should feel calm, protective, and direct.

## Section: How It Works

1. Pick distracting apps or categories.
2. Choose now, later, a date, or a recurring schedule.
3. Confirm the lock before it starts.
4. Bear Lock shields the selected apps until the wake time.

## Section: Why It Works

Bear Lock removes the in-the-moment negotiation. You make the decision before the hard part, and the app keeps that promise visible.

Key points:

- No in-app early unlock.
- Clear wake time.
- Designed for self-control, not surveillance.
- Quiet branding instead of guilt-heavy productivity pressure.

## Section: Privacy

Bear Lock MVP is local-only.

- No account.
- No cloud sync.
- No ads.
- No analytics.
- App selections and schedules stay on device.

## Section: Screenshots

Recommended early screenshot set:

- Setup: brand and permission entry.
- Home: selected target count and lock composer.
- Confirmation: final commitment moment.
- Active Lock: sleeping bear, countdown, no unlock button.
- Diagnostics: local troubleshooting.

## Section: FAQ

Questions to include:

- Why does Bear Lock need Screen Time permission?
- Can I unlock early?
- Does Bear Lock see which apps I select?
- Does Bear Lock support iPad?
- What if a lock does not end correctly?

Answer direction for iPad:

The first release is designed and officially supported for iPhone. It may run on iPad in iPhone compatibility mode, but iPad-specific layouts are not part of the first release.

## Tone

- Calm.
- Clear.
- Trustworthy.
- Slightly playful, but not childish.
- Avoid productivity-shame language.

## Implementation Notes

When building the actual LP, keep it as a real product page rather than a generic marketing splash. First viewport should show the product name, a direct promise, and a real Bear Lock visual. Use screenshots or brand art as bitmap assets.
