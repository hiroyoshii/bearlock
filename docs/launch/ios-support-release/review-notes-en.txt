This version adds optional consumable purchases that support Bear Lock development.

To test the purchases:
1. Open Bear Lock and complete the Screen Time setup if prompted.
2. Open Settings > Support.
3. Select Coffee, Lunch, or Dinner and complete the purchase.
4. Confirm that a thank-you message appears.

All three products are consumables and can be purchased again. They do not unlock features or change any app lock or schedule.

Product IDs:
com.hiyozoo.bearlock.support.coffee
com.hiyozoo.bearlock.support.lunch
com.hiyozoo.bearlock.support.dinner

The button preceding the Screen Time permission request remains "Continue".
